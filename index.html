    About this project

    An independent student prototype exploring USC's University Park Campus in 3D. This is not an official USC map.

    Our campus model: Building footprints downloaded from OpenStreetMap, clipped and prepared in GeoLibre, then displayed with CesiumJS.

    Accuracy: The current model uses estimated building heights, generally 12 metres. The study-area boundary and landmark locations are approximate.

    Other layers: Cesium World Terrain and Cesium OSM Buildings are streamed through Cesium ion.

    Building footprints: © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License.

    Tools: GeoLibre and CesiumJS.
